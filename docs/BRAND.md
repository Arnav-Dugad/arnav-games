# Arnav Games brand

The identity for the app formerly called VYSTRAL. Everything here is generated by scripts from one parametric drawing, so edit the scripts and never hand-edit the files they generate. The exploration and the reasoning behind the choice are in [`assets/brand/concepts/`](../assets/brand/concepts/README.md), and every deliverable is shown together in `assets/brand/concepts/final-board.png`.

## The mark: "Ascent"

Two blades lean together into an A. The left blade is lit and the right is in shade, and a four-point star forms the bar. The two blades stand for your stores and libraries meeting at one peak. The star is the point of light the brand has always had: VYSTRAL's V turned over, with the star moving from its mouth to the A's bar. Every star flourish in the app's motion (the intro, the mode-switch flare) still belongs.

### Construction (master, 512 grid)

| Part | Geometry |
|---|---|
| Apex | (256, 96). It is a true point that overshoots the cap line, as a pointed letter should. |
| Baseline | y = 404. The symbol is 308 tall and 320 wide (outer feet at x = 96 and 416). |
| Blades | Each foot is 84 wide (inner feet at 180 and 332). The inner edges meet at (256, 214), 38.3% down from the apex. |
| Facet seam | The vertical line x = 256 from the apex to the inner apex. It is painted, not drawn: the full silhouette in the lit paint, with the shaded blade laid over it, so the seam never shows a hairline gap. |
| Star | Centred at (256, 330), 76% down from the apex. Its vertical radius is 38 (11.9% of the symbol's width) and its horizontal arms are 86% of that, so it sits in the narrowing counter. The concave sides use a pinch of 0.22. |
| Optical centring | In the tile, the bounding box is centred 6 units above the tile's middle. A triangle's weight sits low, so this puts its mass in the centre. |

The proportions are relative, so every size is the same construction: `geometry()` in `ui/scripts/render-brand-arnav.mjs`.

### Small sizes (hinted)

16, 20, 24, 32, 40 and 48 px have their own drawings (`HINTED` in the script; SVGs in `assets/brand/icon-hinted/`):
- The baseline and outer feet sit on whole pixels.
- At even sizes, the facet seam falls exactly on a pixel boundary, so the two tones stay crisp.
- The symbol fills more of the tile (75% of the width at 16 px, against 62.5% for the master), and the feet are relatively heavier.
- The star has a minimum radius of 1.7 to 2 px and a blunter pinch. Below 32 px it collapses into a bright bar, so the icon still reads as an "A".
- There is no blade halo or star glow below 64 px, and no rim light below 32 px. The tile's aura stays at every size.

## Wordmark

**ARNAV** is set in Geist 600 and **GAMES** in Geist 360, all caps, tracked +0.26em, with a 0.30em word space. Geist's pointed caps echo the apex, and the bold and light weights echo the lit and shaded blades.

The kerning is the font's own, applied by HarfBuzz, plus optical corrections for tracked caps: AV −38, GA −14, NA −12, AM −8, AR −6, ES −6, RN +4, ME +4 (units per 1000 em).

The text is outlined to paths by `ui/scripts/outline-wordmark.py`, so no brand file depends on a font. Geist is SIL OFL 1.1 and is already bundled with the app.

## Lockups

- **Horizontal.** The cap height is 30% of the symbol's height. The caps are centred 4% below the symbol's middle, on the A's visual centre of mass. The gap is 34% of the symbol's height.
- **Stacked.** The wordmark is 2.7× the symbol's width, centred under it, with a gap of 30% of the symbol's height.

The artboards are tight. Add the clear space yourself.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- **Clear space.** Half the symbol's width on every side (160 at the master's 320 width). For lockups, measure from the symbol.
- **App icon (tile).** 16 px, using the hinted drawings. Never scale the 512 drawing down to 16 to 48 px.
- **Bare symbol.** 12 px tall, on dark.
- **Horizontal lockup.** 160 px wide (caps about 9 px).
- **Stacked lockup.** 120 px wide.
- Below these sizes, use the symbol alone.

## Do

- Use the colour symbol on Obsidian or darker, and the `-light` files on light surfaces.
- Use the mono symbol (`currentColor`) for one-colour contexts: High contrast, forced colours, embossing and single-ink print.
- Let the tile's aura and the star's glow be the only effects.
- Keep the star in the counter: it is the brand's signature.

## Don't

- Don't redraw the A in a font, or set the wordmark live in another face.
- Don't recolour the blades in game accent colours. The Living Canvas tints the interface, not the mark.
- Don't swap the lit and shaded sides, flip or rotate the mark, or add outlines, bevels or drop shadows.
- Don't put the colour symbol on mid-tone or busy art without a scrim. Use the mono version instead.
- Don't stretch the wordmark or change its tracking. Use the outlined files.
- Don't use the stacked lockup below 120 px or the horizontal one below 160 px.

## Regenerating

From `ui/`, after `npm ci`:
1. `python scripts/outline-wordmark.py`, only if the wordmark changes. It needs `pip install fonttools brotli uharfbuzz`.
2. `npm run brand:arnav`. This writes every SVG, PNG and ICO above, the splash, the hero and the review board.
3. `node scripts/render-brand-concepts.mjs` and `node scripts/render-wordmark-study.mjs`. These only re-render the exploration sheets.
