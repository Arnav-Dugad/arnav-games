# Arnav Games mark exploration

The brief: rename VYSTRAL to **Arnav Games** with a mark that is ultra-premium, minimal, geometric, memorable and legible from 16 px to 1024 px, and at home in the app's dark Obsidian look with its violet and blue palette.

Each sheet shows every concept as the app tile at 256, 64, 32 and 16 px on dark; as a bare one-ink mark at 64, 32 and 16 px on light; and the true 16 px raster blown up ×6.

- `concept-sheet.png`: round one, ten directions.
- `finalists-sheet.png`: round two, three refinements.
- `wordmark-sheet.png`: the type study.
- `final-board.png`: every final deliverable.

To regenerate (from `ui/`): `node scripts/render-brand-concepts.mjs` and `node scripts/render-wordmark-study.mjs`.

## Round one

| # | Concept | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Monoline**: one continuous stroke | Friendly, but it reads as a handwritten "4" or "A" and looks generic. The stroke ends blur below 32 px. |
| 02 | **Delta**: a softened solid triangle with cut-outs | The clearest at 16 px. Heavy and generic (it is close to several crypto and outdoor brands), not "premium". |
| 03 | **Facet**: two blades lean into an A, with a star as the bar | The most elegant and the most ownable. It inherits VYSTRAL's two blades and star, turned over. The two tones give depth without any effects. **Taken forward.** |
| 04 | **Crest**: a shield with a negative-space A | Reads "esports team" or "security product". The counter fills in at 16 px. |
| 05 | **Orbit**: a peak with an orbit ring | Busy, and it breaks apart below 64 px. |
| 06 | **House of cards**: leaning game cards | A lovely idea (a library that spells A), but the overlaps turn to mush at small sizes. |
| 07 | **AG ligature** | Unreadable below 32 px, and the G fights the A. |
| 08 | **Peak + Play**: a play triangle as the bar | Clever, but the play triangle reads as a stray arrow, and its meaning disappears below 64 px. |
| 09 | **Apex**: a high-contrast geometric A | Typographic and refined, but it is just "a font's A", with no story. |
| 10 | **Split A**: a two-tone solid A | Legible and bold, but heavy. Its facet split went into Facet II. |

## Round two

| # | Concept | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Facet II, star bar** | Wedge blades widen to the foot, which holds weight at small sizes. The apex is tighter, and the star sits optically centred in the counter. **Chosen.** |
| 12 | **Facet II, floating bar** | Quieter, but the bar reads as a dash or minus sign and loses the brand's point of light. |
| 13 | **Star window**: a solid A with a star-shaped counter | Strong at 16 px, but heavy, and at mid sizes the star merges with the notch between the legs. |

## Why "Ascent" (Facet II, star bar)

- **Story.** Two blades, one lit and one in shade, lean together into an A: your stores and libraries meeting at one peak. The four-point star, VYSTRAL's point of light, becomes the A's bar. The brand changes its name, not its soul, and every star flourish in the app's motion (the intro, the mode-switch flare) still belongs.
- **Minimal.** Three shapes, no outlines, and no ornament beyond one gradient per blade. The facet is made by light, not lines.
- **Scales.** The silhouette alone is a confident Λ at 16 px. At 20 to 32 px the star collapses into a bright bar, so the hinted icons still read as an "A". Above 48 px the star resolves into its true shape.
- **Distinct.** Most gaming A marks are solid, shield-like or rounded. Ascent is a sharp, two-tone knife-edge with a light inside it.

## Wordmark

Six options with the chosen mark (see `wordmark-sheet.png`). Unbounded is the app's display face, but it is wide and soft next to the knife-edged mark. Geist's pointed caps match the mark's apex. **ARNAV** at 600 and **GAMES** at 360, tracked +0.26em, mirror the mark's lit and shaded blades.
